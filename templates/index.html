Questions people ask before buying.

Do I need a GPU?

Not for the app, live DSP effects, or passthrough. GPU-backed engines such as Seed-VC realtime and heavy avatar models need a supported NVIDIA CUDA or Apple MPS device; cloud engines are the alternative.

What happens when I reach my daily hours?

The current session stops cleanly and the app explains the reset time. You can keep browsing, change settings, and prepare the next session. The limit resets at 00:00 UTC.

Can I use the app on more than one computer?

Yes, up to the device count in your plan. All devices on one code share the same expiry and daily hours, and you can remove a device in Settings → Account to free its slot.

Which payment methods work?

Paystack and Flutterwave hosted checkout support cards, bank transfer, and USSD where available. We never see or store your card details.
