What counts as studio time?

Only while the pipeline is running and processing. Browsing settings, editing profiles, and previewing a still image do not count.

What resets each day?

Your daily hours reset at 00:00 UTC. Unused hours do not roll over, which is why the longer terms are discounted.

Can I change plans?

Finish the current term, then activate a new code. We do not auto-charge or auto-renew — every purchase is explicit.

How activation works

Pay on this site

Paystack or Flutterwave handles the card, transfer, or USSD payment. We never receive card details.

Copy your code

The success page shows an activation code like ML-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX. Keep it private; it can activate your plan's full device count.

Enter it in the app

Moonlight Studio binds the code to each device and starts the term on the first activation. Every device shares one daily pool.
