How passes work

Pay once

Paystack or Flutterwave handles the transaction. You receive a unique activation code immediately after payment.

Activate on your device

Enter the code in Moonlight Studio. It binds permanently to that one device on first use.

Use your hours

Your lifetime limit counts down whenever the studio pipeline is running. When hours reach zero the pass is fully exhausted — no resets, no renewals.

Can I transfer to another device?

No. A pass binds to the first device that activates it and cannot be moved. Choose the machine you plan to stream from.

What counts as studio time?

Only while the AI pipeline is actively running. Idle preview, settings editing, or paused sessions do not consume your hours.

Do hours expire?

Yes — passes expire 30 days after first activation regardless of how many hours remain, so activate when you are ready to use them.
